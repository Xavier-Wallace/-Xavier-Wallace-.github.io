Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.

Founded

January 9, 1914

Founding Location

Howard University, Washington, D.C.

History

Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. was founded by A. Langston Taylor, Leonard F. Morse, and Charles I. Brown at Howard University. The founders believed that a fraternity should focus not only on itself but also on making a positive impact in the community.

The fraternity was established on the principles of Brotherhood, Scholarship, and Service. Since its founding, Phi Beta Sigma has expanded throughout the United States and internationally.

Members have worked to support educational achievement, leadership development, and service initiatives that benefit local communities.

Impact and Legacy

Phi Beta Sigma has promoted community engagement through mentoring, educational programs, and civic involvement.

The fraternity continues to encourage academic excellence and leadership while helping members become productive citizens.

Today, Phi Beta Sigma remains committed to improving communities through service and brotherhood.
