Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.

Founded

January 13, 1913

Founding Location

Howard University, Washington, D.C.

History

Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc. was founded by 22 collegiate women at Howard University who were committed to public service and social action.

Shortly after its founding, members participated in the Women's Suffrage March of 1913, demonstrating the organization's commitment to advocacy and civic engagement.

The sorority expanded throughout the United States and internationally, creating programs focused on education, health, and community development.

Impact and Legacy

Delta Sigma Theta has played a significant role in promoting educational opportunities and social progress.

Members continue to lead initiatives focused on economic development, voter education, public service, and leadership training.

Today, the sorority remains one of the largest and most influential historically Black organizations in the world.
