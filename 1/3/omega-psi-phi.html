Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc.

Founded

November 17, 1911

Founding Location

Howard University, Washington, D.C.

History

Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. was founded by Edgar Amos Love, Oscar James Cooper, Frank Coleman, and faculty adviser Dr. Ernest Everett Just at Howard University.

The fraternity became the first international fraternal organization founded on the campus of a historically Black college or university. It was built upon the principles of Manhood, Scholarship, Perseverance, and Uplift.

Since 1911, Omega Psi Phi has expanded throughout the United States and internationally while maintaining a strong commitment to service and leadership.

Impact and Legacy

Omega Psi Phi has produced leaders in government, education, athletics, science, and civil rights.

The fraternity sponsors scholarship programs, mentoring initiatives, voter education efforts, and community outreach activities.

Today, Omega Psi Phi continues to uphold its commitment to uplifting communities and developing future leaders.
