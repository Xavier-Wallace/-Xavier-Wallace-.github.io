Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc.

Founded

November 12, 1922

Founding Location

Butler University, Indianapolis, Indiana

History

Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. was founded by seven educators at Butler University. Unlike many Divine Nine organizations, Sigma Gamma Rho was founded at a predominantly white institution.

The founders believed in the importance of education, leadership, and service. They worked to create opportunities for African American women while supporting their communities.

The sorority expanded nationwide and later internationally, building a reputation for academic excellence and civic engagement.

Impact and Legacy

Sigma Gamma Rho sponsors programs focused on education, health awareness, youth development, and community service.

Members continue to serve as leaders in education, business, government, and nonprofit organizations.

Today, the sorority remains dedicated to empowering women and improving communities through service.
