Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc.

Founded

January 15, 1908

Founding Location

Howard University, Washington, D.C.

History

Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. was founded by nine students at Howard University. It became the first historically Black Greek-letter sorority and was created to support academic excellence and leadership among African American women.

The sorority expanded quickly across the United States and established chapters at colleges, universities, and within communities. Members focused on education, service, and uplifting others through organized programs and initiatives.

Throughout its history, Alpha Kappa Alpha has encouraged women to become leaders in their professions while maintaining a strong commitment to scholarship and service.

Impact and Legacy

Alpha Kappa Alpha has made significant contributions through scholarship programs, health initiatives, and community service projects.

The organization has inspired generations of women to pursue higher education and become active leaders within their communities.

Today, Alpha Kappa Alpha remains one of the largest and most influential historically Black sororities in the world.
