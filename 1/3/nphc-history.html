History of the National Pan-Hellenic Council

The National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHC) was founded on May 10, 1930, at Howard University to bring historically Black fraternities and sororities together under a common governing body. The council is commonly known as the Divine Nine. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

The NPHC promotes leadership, scholarship, service, and cooperation among its member organizations while preserving the unique history and traditions of each group. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
