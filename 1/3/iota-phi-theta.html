Iota Phi Theta Fraternity, Inc.

Founded

September 19, 1963

Founding Location

Morgan State University, Baltimore, Maryland

History

Iota Phi Theta Fraternity, Inc. was founded by twelve men at Morgan State University during the height of the Civil Rights Movement. Many of the founders were older students, military veterans, and working professionals.

The fraternity was established to address the social and political issues affecting African Americans while promoting leadership, scholarship, citizenship, fidelity, and brotherhood.

Iota Phi Theta experienced steady growth and was officially recognized as the ninth member organization of the National Pan-Hellenic Council in 1997.

Impact and Legacy

Iota Phi Theta has focused on youth mentoring, leadership development, and community service programs.

Members continue to support educational initiatives and projects that strengthen communities across the country.

Today, the fraternity remains committed to its founding principles while continuing to expand its impact nationwide.
