About the Divine Nine

The Divine Nine is the common name for the nine historically Black Greek-letter organizations that make up the National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHC).

These organizations have histories connected to education, leadership, fellowship, scholarship, and community service. Their members have contributed to campuses and communities across the United States.
