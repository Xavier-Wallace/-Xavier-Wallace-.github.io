Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc.

Founded

January 16, 1920

Founding Location

Howard University, Washington, D.C.

History

Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc. was founded by five women at Howard University who believed that scholarship, service, sisterhood, and finer womanhood should guide the organization.

The sorority became the first historically Black Greek-letter organization to establish a constitutional bond with a brother fraternity through its relationship with Phi Beta Sigma.

Since its founding, Zeta Phi Beta has expanded nationally and internationally while maintaining a commitment to education, leadership, and community service.

Impact and Legacy

Zeta Phi Beta has developed programs that focus on health, education, youth development, and family support.

Members actively serve their communities through volunteer efforts, mentoring, and leadership initiatives.

Today, the organization continues to empower women and strengthen communities through service and scholarship.
