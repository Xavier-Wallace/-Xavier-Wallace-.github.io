Welcome

The Divine Nine refers to the nine historically Black Greek-letter organizations that make up the National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHC). These organizations have played a major role in scholarship, leadership, community service, and civil rights efforts throughout American history.

This website explores the founding, growth, and lasting impact of these organizations while highlighting their contributions to higher education and communities across the country.

About the National Pan-Hellenic Council

The National Pan-Hellenic Council was established in 1930 at Howard University to promote cooperation among historically Black fraternities and sororities. Today, the council represents nine organizations known collectively as the Divine Nine.

The Divine Nine Organizations

  • Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc.
  • Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc.
  • Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc.
  • Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc.
  • Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.
  • Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.
  • Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc.
  • Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc.
  • Iota Phi Theta Fraternity, Inc.
