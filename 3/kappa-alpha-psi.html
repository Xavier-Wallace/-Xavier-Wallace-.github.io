Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc.

Founded

January 5, 1911

Founding Location

Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana

History

Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. was founded by ten students at Indiana University. The fraternity was established during a period when African American students faced many social and educational challenges.

The founders created the organization to encourage achievement in every field of human endeavor. As membership grew, chapters were established throughout the country.

The fraternity continues to focus on leadership, scholarship, brotherhood, and professional development.

Impact and Legacy

Kappa Alpha Psi has helped develop leaders in business, government, education, athletics, and public service.

Members participate in mentoring programs, scholarship initiatives, and community service projects that positively impact society.

Today, the fraternity remains committed to achievement, leadership, and service.
