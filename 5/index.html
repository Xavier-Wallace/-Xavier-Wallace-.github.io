Discover the Divine Nine

The Divine Nine represents nine historically Black Greek-letter organizations that have made lasting contributions to education, leadership, community service, and culture.

What Is the Divine Nine?

The Divine Nine is the collective name for the nine organizations that make up the National Pan-Hellenic Council. Each organization has its own history, traditions, and commitment to serving its community.
